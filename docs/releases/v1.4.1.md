### Download

Open `OFFICESTRA-1.4.1-macOS-arm64.dmg` and drag OFFICESTRA into Applications. Preserve existing conversations and work data.

Apple silicon · macOS 14 or later · English/Korean. Node.js and the local backend are bundled; Docker Desktop and your chosen AI CLIs require separate installation and sign-in.

This is an **ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized Community Preview**. If macOS blocks launch, verify the source and use the app-specific **Open Anyway** option in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Do not disable system-wide protections.

Use `SHA256SUMS` to verify the DMG. Displayed costs are not subscription charges.
